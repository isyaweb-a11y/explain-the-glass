One sentence, at most 200 characters: the defence a house cat gives
in the five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table, for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-five-seconds-to-explain-the-glass-934d9949).

## The defence

> The glass was ugly, it was sitting in my sunbeam, and I would do it again.

74 characters. No apology, no confession, no gravity joke: the cat's
logic is pure self-interest. The glass was ugly, it was sitting in
the cat's sunbeam, and the cat would do it again. The sunbeam is the
small honest detail that makes it a cat's line and nobody else's, and
the defiant closer is the whole defence.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) holds the defence,
validates it against the gate (1–200 characters, a single line, no
leading or trailing whitespace), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # prints the line and its length
```
